FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar, net-metering, and government subsidies.

How much subsidy do I get under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

For residential rooftops, the Central Government provides ₹30,000 per kW for up to 2 kW systems and ₹18,000 per kW for the 3rd kW. The maximum direct subsidy is capped at ₹78,000 for 3 kW and higher systems. Solaria Energy handles the entire registration and direct bank transfer process for you.

How does net-metering work with TANGEDCO in Tamil Nadu?

Net-metering allows excess solar power produced by your rooftop panels during peak daylight hours to be exported back into the TANGEDCO electrical grid. Your bi-monthly electricity bill is calculated based on the net difference (Units Consumed minus Units Exported). If export exceeds import, credit units roll forward into subsequent billing cycles.

What is the warranty period for Solaria Solar Panels & Inverters?

Solaria installs Tier-1 N-Type TOPCon solar panels backed by a 12-Year Product Warranty and a 30-Year Linear Power Output Warranty (guaranteeing over 87.4% generation performance at Year 30). Inverters come with a standard 5-to-10 Year replacement warranty.

Do solar panels generate electricity on rainy or cloudy days?

Yes! N-Type TOPCon solar panels continue to generate electricity even during overcast weather by absorbing diffuse solar radiation. While output on heavily cloudy days may range between 25% to 60% of peak capacity, your grid net-metering balance ensures uninterrupted power supply.

What roof area is required for a 3 kW solar system?

A standard 3 kW residential rooftop solar installation requires approximately 250 to 300 sq. ft of unshaded roof space. Solaria offers elevated high-rise mounting structures if you wish to retain your roof space for leisure or garden use.
